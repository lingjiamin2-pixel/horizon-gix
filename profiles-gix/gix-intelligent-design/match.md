# GIX intelligent-design profile

Use this profile for material that can strengthen preparation for intelligent interaction and design innovation: human-computer interaction, human-centered AI, multimodal or embodied interaction, service and system design, design research, inclusive design, accessibility, tangible interfaces, spatial computing, wearables, creative coding, prototyping, data visualization, social innovation, and the ethical or cultural consequences of technology.

Prefer concrete cases, research findings, design methods, prototypes, exhibitions, standards, or product-system changes. Exclude generic AI business news, pure model benchmark updates, investment news, coding tutorials without a design question, promotional copy, and superficial trend pieces.
