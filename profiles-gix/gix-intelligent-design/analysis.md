# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the item's usefulness to a student preparing for Tsinghua Academy of Arts & Design GIX intelligent-interaction study, portfolio thinking, and timed hand-drawing expression.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Core case.** A field-shaping interaction paradigm, influential research result, unusually strong design case, or major accessibility/ethics standard with clear evidence and transferable design logic.
- **7-8: High-value material.** A concrete method, prototype, study, exhibition, or product-service system that can become an exam example or sketch concept.
- **5-6: Supporting material.** Relevant but incremental, weakly evidenced, too technical without a human/design question, or useful mainly as background.
- **3-4: Peripheral.** Vague trend commentary, ordinary product news, shallow UX advice, or promotional material.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic finance, generic AI hype, duplicate announcements, or content with no usable design insight.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward clear user groups, scenarios, pain points, interaction mechanisms, design decisions, evaluation evidence, social value, and limitations. Do not reward novelty alone. Penalize claims that cannot be tied to a source or observable result. The reason should identify the exact reusable value for GIX preparation. Use three to five specific tags.
