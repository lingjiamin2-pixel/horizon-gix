# Art and design history-theory profile

Use this profile for material with clear historical or theoretical value in Chinese and global art, architecture, craft, visual culture, industrial design, graphic design, modern design movements, museum collections, exhibitions, archives, conservation, criticism, and the social or technological conditions of design.

Prefer primary institutions, museum or archive research, scholarly interpretation, newly available evidence, and cases that enable comparison across periods or cultures. Exclude event listings, auction-price news, celebrity coverage, decorative image galleries without analysis, generic lifestyle content, and claims that lack historical context.
