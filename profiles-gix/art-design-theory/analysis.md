# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the item's usefulness as supplementary evidence for the 621 Chinese and foreign art/design history and theory exam. This radar supplements systematic textbook study; it does not replace it.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional evidence.** Important archival evidence, a major reinterpretation, or a well-supported exhibition/research project that clarifies a canonical movement, figure, work, institution, or cross-cultural exchange.
- **7-8: Strong answer material.** A credible case with precise dates, works, concepts, formal features, social context, or comparative value usable in a definition, short answer, or essay.
- **5-6: Background.** Historically relevant but narrow, repetitive, weakly contextualized, or useful mainly for visual memory.
- **3-4: Low utility.** General exhibition promotion, listicle treatment, market news, or description without analysis.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic, inaccurate, sensational, or purely commercial content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward chronology, named works and makers, formal or material analysis, institutional context, technology and production conditions, cross-cultural comparison, and explicit evidence. Penalize presentism and unsupported links to canonical history. The reason must state whether the item is best for a term definition, short answer, essay example, or visual-memory card. Use three to five specific tags.
